Quick answers

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 24 hours and usually arrive in 2–5 working days.

Can I return sneakers?

Yes — unworn pairs can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

How do I choose a size?

Our shoes fit true to size. If you are between sizes, take the larger one.

Is checkout real?

No. This is a student demo: checkout simulates an order and nothing is charged.
