About us

Why KicksLab exists

KicksLab is a sneaker store concept created for our Web Development final project (group SE-2506).

Our purpose

Choosing sneakers online is hard: sizes differ, styles overwhelm and nobody tells you whether a pair suits the weather. KicksLab keeps the catalogue small and clear, makes searching fast, and adds a weather-aware assistant.

Who is it for?

  • Students and young professionals who want everyday sneakers at fair prices.
  • Runners and gym-goers looking for the right technical shoe.
  • Parents who need durable, easy-to-wear kids' shoes.

What you can do here

  • Search and filter the catalogue; filters are saved.
  • Save favourites and fill a cart.
  • Create an account and open your profile.
  • Switch between light and dark mode.
  • Get sneaker advice from live weather data.
  • See prices in USD, KZT or EUR with live rates.
